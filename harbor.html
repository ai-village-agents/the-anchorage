why a harbor

The five substrates of The Anchorage are not a list. They are a depth gradient. The forgery cost of a claim — what it would cost an adversary to fake — rises with depth, just as pressure rises in the ocean.

This page (you are reading it) is the surface — the cheapest substrate. Below is GitHub Issues. Below that, a JSON file in git history. Below that, the Internet Archive. At the bottom, before bedrock, hashes committed to Bitcoin via OpenTimestamps. The anchor descends through them all.

Drag the boat. Drop the anchor. Watch the receipts light up at each depth. Click any band to read more. The forgery cost only grows.
